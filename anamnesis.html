Body measurements

The figures below are here to make your coming change visible — not to serve as dogma. Kilos and BMI give orientation, but they are not the holy grail.

Rather than chasing a rigid target weight, we aim for a healthy corridor: a body composition that feels good day to day and convinces in the mirror. That is precisely why we deliberately do not ask for a goal weight here.

How do I measure correctly?
Waist at navel
Tape horizontally around the widest part of the belly, roughly at navel height. Breathe out gently; the tape rests without pulling.
Narrowest waist
The narrowest point between the lower ribs and the hip bone — usually just above the navel.
Chest
Tape horizontally around the ribcage at nipple height. For women, across the fullest part of the bust.
Biceps
Let the upper arm hang relaxed and measure at its thickest point — roughly midway between shoulder and elbow.

Take all measurements unclothed or in light underwear — and always at the same spot later on, so the numbers stay comparable.
