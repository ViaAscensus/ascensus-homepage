My approach

Why Ascensus

Hi, I'm Patrick — and I know the diet spiral from personal experience. I used to try a lot of things: from extreme crash diets to excessive exercise. In the short term, it worked. After a few weeks, the kilos were back. And despite all the exercise, I often just felt drained, because my nutrition didn't fit.

I thought: only extreme approaches get results. That road kept leading me back to the same point — “I simply can't keep up these extremes in the long run.”

It's not the extreme that wins. It's what fits you, and what you can keep building into your life.

That's why I always start with an honest analysis of where you are today. Only those who understand where they stand can start in the right place. I work with data instead of gut feeling, as equals instead of lecturing — and without lists of forbidden foods. The dose makes the poison, not the individual food.

In the end, what matters is not the number on the scale. What matters is your well-being and how well you perform in everyday life.

The principle behind it

Measure first.
Then judge.

In the first week, the calorie and nutrient display in your app is deliberately hidden. You track — but you don't see any numbers.

That sounds odd at first, but it's the most important step. As soon as numbers are visible, eating behavior changes automatically: you eat a little better, skip dessert, don't log the cookie. In the end you have a week that looks nicer than real life — and a plan built on an illusion.

Without the display, you get an unfiltered picture. Only after our joint review do I fully unlock your dashboard.

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to come to Frankfurt for the coaching?

No. The entire collaboration happens online — strategy session, review and live calls by video. You can work with me anywhere in Germany, regardless of where you live.

In which language does the coaching take place?

The coaching is available in German and English. Just tell me in the free intro call which language you prefer.

What exactly is blind tracking?

In the first week you keep tracking your food as usual, but without calorie or nutrient values being displayed. This gives an unfiltered picture of your real habits, instead of you unconsciously eating whatever looks good. Only then do we review together and unlock the full values.

What does coaching with ASCENSUS cost?

Packages start at €149 (The Foundation, 3 weeks) and go up to €357 for 3 months of continuous support (Sustainability, also available in 3 installments of €119). All prices are final prices; no VAT is shown under § 19 UStG (German small-business regulation). The full breakdown is on the packages page.

What happens in the free intro call?

30 minutes, no obligation and no sales pressure. We clarify where you stand right now, what is on your mind, and whether and how I can support you — afterwards you decide in peace whether it fits.

Do you work with lists of forbidden foods or strict diet plans?

No, deliberately not. There are no crash diets, no blanket bans and no dogma. We look at your everyday life without judgment and, step by step, develop a way of eating you can truly sustain — the dose makes the poison, not the individual food.

Do you also offer running coaching?

Running coaching and a combined hybrid package of nutrition and running are in preparation and cannot be booked yet. Feel free to get in touch if you would like to be notified as soon as it is available.
