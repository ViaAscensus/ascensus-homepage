Oven-Roasted Vegetables with Crème Fraîche

509 kcal · 9 g protein · 55 g carbohydrates · 25 g fat per serving

15 min Vegetarian High-fibre
servings 15 min preparation 509 kcal / serving

Ingredients

  • Carrots600 g
  • Celeriac375 g
  • Ciabatta0.5 pcs
  • Crème fraîche200 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 sprig
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Pepper1 tsp
  • Salt2 tsp
  • Sweet paprika1 tbsp
  • Waxy potatoes750 g

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C (390 °F). Wash the potatoes and carrots, peel the celeriac and cut everything into wedges. Finely chop the garlic.
  2. Put the vegetables in a bowl with the paprika, salt, pepper, garlic and 2 tbsp of olive oil and mix everything well.
  3. Spread the vegetables on a baking tray lined with aluminium foil and bake them for 20 minutes in the middle of the oven. Turn them with a spatula and bake for another 15 minutes.
  4. In the meantime, cut the ciabatta into bite-sized pieces. Reduce the oven temperature to 175 °C (350 °F), mix the bread into the vegetables, drizzle the remaining oil over it and finish everything in 10 minutes.
  5. Pluck the rosemary needles from the sprig and sprinkle them over the finished vegetables. Serve with the crème fraîche.

Allergens: Celery, Gluten, Milk
