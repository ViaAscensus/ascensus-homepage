Week by week

What happens in the packages

So you know what you're getting into — here is the complete process.

The Foundation 3 weeks · €149
Week 1

Initial consultation & real-life tracking

In the 45-minute strategy session we go through your anamnesis and your goals. Afterwards you log your food in the app for one week — completely as usual, without having to change anything.

Week 2

Analysis & potential analysis

I analyze your data scientifically and show you in the digital closing session where your strengths lie, where the gaps are, and which levers will make the biggest difference for you.

Weeks 2–3

Follow-up app access

After the analysis you keep the app for two more weeks — now with all nutrition displays, so you can apply what you've learned right away.

The Path 4 weeks · €199
Week 1

The honest status quo analysis

45 minutes of initial consultation on your starting point, goals and daily life. Then seven days of live tracking without calorie and nutrient display — for an uninfluenced data basis without psychological pressure.

Week 2

Analysis & plan creation

Scientific analysis of your tracking week. From it we develop your individual nutrition plan together, matched to your goal — body fat reduction, more energy in everyday life or muscle gain — and to your daily routine.

Weeks 3–4

Supported implementation in everyday life

A 15-minute live call every week: discuss successes, solve everyday hurdles, fine-tune. Plus full app access with all nutrition analyses, recipes and shopping lists for the entire remaining time.

Sustainability 12 weeks · €357
Week 1

The blind status quo analysis

45-minute initial session to establish your goals and daily life, then seven days of tracking without nutrient feedback — so your unfiltered habits become visible.

Week 2

Analysis & tailor-made plan

Scientific analysis of your data including needs calculation. Together we create your practical nutrition plan. From now on all app features, nutrition values, recipes and shopping lists are unlocked.

Weeks 3–11

The supported implementation phase

Weekly 15-minute calls in which we catch everyday hurdles early and discuss progress. I adjust your calorie and macronutrient targets in the app the day after our call if needed.

Week 12

The closing session

Digital milestone appointment: we look back on your journey and work out your strategic roadmap, so you can keep up your routine on your own in the long term.
